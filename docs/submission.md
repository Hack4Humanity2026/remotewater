# Submission drafts (web form, due 3:30 PM 2026-09-26)

Character limits are from the participant guide. Problem areas: Water Safety and Water Delivery. Prototype link: https://hack4humanity2026.github.io/remotewater/

## Brief description (max 250)

In June 2026 every family in Inukjuak was told to boil their water, with no end date, because no one could say which truckload was in their tank. RemoteWater gives each home one answer, in minutes, in Inuktitut, offline: is the water in my tank safe?

## How it addresses the problem (max 700)

A truck comes to each home in Inukjuak every day or two and fills the tank inside. When a lab in Montreal phones to say the plant's water failed, nobody can say which homes got that water, so everyone is told to boil and nobody is told when it ends. RemoteWater fixes that with three small habits. The operator notes the chlorine reading they already take each morning. The driver taps Delivered at each door. The lab taps pass or fail. From those taps, one failed test tells every affected family within minutes: on their phone at home, on the village Facebook group, on the radio, and at the door from the driver for homes with no phone. When clean water reaches a tank, that family is told.

## Social impact (max 700)

1,821 people live in Inukjuak, and most speak Inuktitut at home. When the water goes bad, a mother making formula, an elder living alone and a teacher with a classroom all get the same answer today: boil everything, indefinitely, and listen to the radio for news. The school and the health centre close when the water is not safe. RemoteWater gives each household its own answer, in its own language, within minutes of the lab call, and tells them the day their own tank is clean again. Nobody is left out: a home with no phone hears it from the driver they already know. The same thing works in every one of Nunavik's 14 villages, and nothing has to be installed in anyone's house.

## Unique value (max 700)

Others put a sensor in every tank. A sensor needs a technician, and Inukjuak has no mechanic of its own. RemoteWater needs no new equipment at all. It uses the people and the trips that already exist: the operator's morning reading, the driver's daily rounds and the lab's phone call. The truck carries the information, so it works with no cell signal, on home WiFi or at the door. It reaches people where they already are: the phone, the village Facebook group, the community radio and the driver. It grows a village at a time with one file of houses, run by the regional office that already collects every village's weekly test. Every number is marked sourced, assumed or hypothetical.
