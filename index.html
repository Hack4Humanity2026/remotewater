Nunatsiaq News, 2026-06-11 · Statistics Canada, 2021 Census

No pipes. One plant. Three trucks.

A tank in every home, filled every day or two.

Water is tested at the plant, then never seen again until the tap.

What RemoteWater does

Every fill is a batch. Every delivery is logged.

One failed test tells each home in minutes, in Inuktitut, with no signal and nothing to install.

Home WiFi, the village Facebook group, the radio, and the driver at the door.
