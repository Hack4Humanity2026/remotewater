Is the water in my tank safe?

Hack for Humanity · Ottawa · 2026-09-26 · Josh & Souheil

June 2026, Inukjuak, northern Quebec

E. coli was found in the town's drinking water.

1,800 people were told to boil their water.
Nobody said when it would end.

And nobody could say which homes had the bad water in their tank.

Nunatsiaq News, 2026-06-11 · Statistics Canada, 2021 Census

Inukjuak has no water pipes.

Water is cleaned at one plant. A truck drives it to a tank inside each home. Once it leaves the plant, nobody checks it again.

How RemoteWater works

Four small steps. Nothing new to install.

1
The plant writes down today's water reading.
It already does this every morning. Each truckload gets that day's number.
2
The driver taps Delivered at each door.
Works with no signal. Syncs when the truck is back at the plant.
3
The lab says pass or fail.
If it fails, we know exactly which homes got that water.
4
Every home is told, in minutes.
On the phone, on Facebook, on the radio, or by the driver at the door.
✓ Safe⚠ Boil

Every family knows about their own tank.

In minutes. In Inuktitut. With no cell signal. With nothing installed in the house.

And when clean water arrives, each home is told it is safe again, one by one.
